# Privacy Policy for the "Zero Balance: Credit Cleaner" Application

**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

Sergey Kotov (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer", "we", "us") respects the privacy of users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the "Zero Balance: Credit Cleaner" mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application").

## 1. How the Application Works and What Data We Process

The Application is designed to help users zero out their Apple ID account balance (by making an in-app purchase) to subsequently change their region in Apple settings.

**Important:** The Application does not receive or store your Apple ID password, nor does it have access to your bank card data. All financial transactions are processed solely by Apple Inc. through the In-App Purchase (StoreKit) system.

We may collect the following categories of data:
- **Technical Data:** Device model, operating system version (iOS), unique device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV) for analytics and fraud prevention.
- **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the Application (e.g., button clicks, session time), as well as data about completed in-app purchases (StoreKit receipts).
- **Diagnostic Data:** Crash and error reports to improve the Application's performance.

We **DO NOT collect**:
- Your personal data (name, address, phone number).
- Apple ID passwords.
- Bank card data or any other payment details.

## 2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely for:
- Ensuring the core functionality of the Application (making an in-app purchase to zero out the balance).
- Analyzing stability and troubleshooting errors.
- Improving user experience and interface.

## 3. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data sharing is only possible in the following cases:
- **Apple Inc.:** For processing in-app purchases and confirming transactions.
- **Analytics Services:** If third-party SDKs (e.g., Google Firebase, AppsFlyer) are used in the Application to collect anonymized statistics.
- **By Law:** If required to comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access. Since the Application does not collect confidential financial information, leakage risks are minimized. All transactions pass through Apple's secure servers.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the age established by the legislation of your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to request information about what data is stored about you, as well as to request its deletion. To do so, please contact us using the contact details below.
